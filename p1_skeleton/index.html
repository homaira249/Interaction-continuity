User-Controlled Multimodal Desktop Environment

Rethinking How Humans Interact With Computers

Complete three everyday desktop tasks with your mouse, your keyboard, your voice, or any mix of the three. You decide when to switch. The system never does it for you.

Open Workspace Open Input Studio

Any task, any modality

Each task works with every modality, so you can finish it whichever way suits you.

Three tasks

  • File organizationSort files into the right folders.
  • Application navigationOpen, switch between and close apps.
  • Desktop settingsSet the theme, volume and brightness.

Three modalities

  • MousePoint, click, drag and drop.
  • KeyboardShortcuts, arrow keys and Tab.
  • VoiceSpoken commands, using your browser's speech recognition.

You stay in control

  • Manual switching

    Modalities change only when you change them.

  • Progress preserved

    Selections and unfinished actions survive every switch.

  • Consent first

    Tracking is optional and starts only after you agree.

Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition, which can send audio to a remote server. You will see the details before you first use voice.
